Why We Register Your Stay

Zürich · Legal background, in English

This is an English summary of the City of Zürich's information for accommodation providers («Für Beherbergende»). The authoritative version is the official page of the City of Zürich (German only).

Who must keep a guest register

Anyone providing accommodation in Zürich — whether a hotel, a business, or a private person hosting guests regularly — is required to keep a guest register («Gästekontrolle») and to make this data available to the police for the purpose of identity verification. This also applies to private hosts who rent out their apartment commercially through online booking platforms such as Airbnb.

Registration duty

Guest reports must be submitted electronically. The necessary registration forms are provided by the Cantonal Police of Zürich (hotelkontrolle@kapo.zh.ch).

Legal basis

Police Act of the Canton of Zürich (PolG; LS 550.1, § 21 para. 4): accommodation providers must keep a guest register and issue registration forms («Meldescheine»), which are to be made available to the police for identity verification.

Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (FNIA/AIG; SR 142.20, Art. 16): anyone who provides commercial accommodation to foreign nationals must report them to the competent cantonal authority.

What this means for you as a guest

Your host is legally required to record your name, nationality, and ID document details and report your stay. This is the same procedure every hotel in Switzerland follows at check-in. The registration form on this site simply lets you provide those details securely to your host.
